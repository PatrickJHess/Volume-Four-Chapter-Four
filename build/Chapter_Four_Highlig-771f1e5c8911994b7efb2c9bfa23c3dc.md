# ✨ Chapter Highlights

## 🎯 Key Topics Covered

**Defining and Evaluating Duration and Convexity as Measures of Risk**
* **Present Value Basis:** Duration and convexity follow directly from the present value formula.
* **Duration:** The first derivative of the present value with respect to the single discount rate (YTM).
* **Convexity:** The second derivative of the present value with respect to the single discount rate (YTM). 
* **Risk Measurement:** These are mathematical concepts with obvious implications for measuring the impact of interest rate risk.
* **Dynamic Adjustments:** Both measures are dynamic and change with shifts in the shape, level, and curvature of the term structure of interest rates.
* **Strategic Simulations:** Simulations demonstrate how beliefs about changes in the term structure affect how duration and convexity are used to control risk and develop investment strategies.
