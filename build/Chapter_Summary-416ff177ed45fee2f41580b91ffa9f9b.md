# 📝📉 Chapter Summary

<br>



Risk is at the heart of financial markets—indeed, their *raison d'être*. Notwithstanding its importance, this chapter is our first discussion of risk and financial assets, limited here to a mathematical result that points to a way of measuring potential hazards. 

**Key Objectives & Takeaways:**
* **📐 Core Concepts:** We explored the foundational principles of duration and convexity, illustrating how these metrics are essential for controlling risk and defining fixed-income strategies.
* **🔄 Dynamic Nature:** A central theme is that duration and convexity are not static, "one and done" measurements. They are highly dynamic, requiring the continuous tuning of risk management protocols and investment positions.
* **💻 Term Structure Simulations:** Our most powerful illustrations relied on term structure simulations, carefully designed to emulate the decision-making process of an informed investor. 
* **🧠 Embedding Beliefs:** We bridged theory and practice by filtering uninformed simulations through **Beta distributions** to embed specific market beliefs—demonstrating exactly how an investor uses duration and convexity as the foundation of a real-world trading strategy.
