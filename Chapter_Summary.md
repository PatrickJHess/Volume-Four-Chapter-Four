### 📖 Chapter Summary

Risk is at the heart of financial markets—indeed, their *raison d'être*. Notwithstanding its importance, this chapter is our first discussion of risk and financial assets, limited here to a mathematical result that points to a way of measuring potential hazards. 

**Key Objectives & Takeaways:**
* **Core Concepts:** Describes the basic principles of **duration** and **convexity**, illustrating how these measures fit into controlling risk and defining investment strategies.
* **Dynamic Nature:** The main message is that duration and convexity are not "one and done" measures. They are dynamic, calling for the frequent tuning of risk management and investment strategies.
* **Term Structure Simulations:** Our most powerful illustrations are simulations of the term structure, designed to emulate an informed investor. 
* **Embedding Beliefs:** The bridge is to filter uninformed simulations with **Beta distributions** that embed beliefs—a demonstration of how an investor would use duration and convexity as the foundation of an investment strategy.
